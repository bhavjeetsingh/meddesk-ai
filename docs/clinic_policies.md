# CareFirst Medical Center - Clinic Policies

## Cancellation Policy
- **24-hour notice** is required for all cancellations
- Late cancellations (less than 24 hours) may incur a **₹500 fee**
- **No-shows** may result in a **₹1,000 fee**
- Three or more no-shows may result in discharge from the practice
- Cancellations can be done via phone, WhatsApp, or in-person

## Payment Policy
- Payment is due at **time of service**
- **GST of 18%** is applicable on consultation fees
- We accept: Cash, UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking
- For insurance patients, co-payment may apply
- Self-pay patients can claim reimbursement from insurer

## Insurance Policy
- We offer **cashless treatment** for most major insurance providers
- Please bring your **insurance card** and **photo ID** (Aadhaar/PAN/Passport)
- Pre-authorization may be required for certain procedures
- For government schemes, please bring your scheme card

## Prescription Policy
- Prescription refills require **48-hour notice**
- Please contact your pharmacy first for refills
- New prescriptions require a consultation appointment
- Online prescriptions available via teleconsultation

## Medical Records Policy
- Medical records requests take **5-7 business days** to process
- A **₹200 administrative fee** may apply
- Records can be collected in-person or sent via email
- ABHA (Ayushman Bharat Health Account) integration available

## Teleconsultation Policy
- Available for follow-ups and non-urgent consultations
- **₹800 flat fee** per consultation
- Requires stable internet connection
- Camera and microphone required
- Prescription provided digitally after consultation

## Emergency Policy
- For life-threatening emergencies, call **108 (Ambulance)** or **102**
- Clinic emergency line: **+91 98765 43210**
- Nearest hospitals listed in patient handbook
- We do not handle trauma cases - please go to nearest hospital

## Privacy Policy
- All patient information is kept confidential
- We comply with Indian Medical Council (MCI) regulations
- ABHA (Ayushman Bharat Health Account) for digital health records
- Patient consent required for sharing medical records

## Patient Rights
- Right to respectful, non-discriminatory care
- Right to privacy and confidentiality
- Right to informed consent
- Right to access medical records
- Right to request a second opinion
- Right to file a grievance

## Patient Responsibilities
- Provide accurate health information
- Follow agreed-upon treatment plans
- Keep appointments or cancel with proper notice
- Treat staff and other patients with respect
- Ask questions when you don't understand your care

## Grievance Redressal
- For complaints, speak to the front desk or call +91 98765 43210
- Written complaints can be emailed to: complaints@carefirstmedical.in
- We aim to resolve all complaints within 7 working days

- **Sunday:** 10:00 AM - 2:00 PM
- **Public Holidays:** Closed
