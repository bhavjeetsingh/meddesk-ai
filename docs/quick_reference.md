## Common Patient Questions

**Q: Do you accept my insurance?**
A: We accept most major Indian insurance providers. Please provide your insurance provider name for verification.

**Q: What are the consultation fees?**
A: General consultation: ₹1,000 | Cardiology: ₹1,500 (GST additional)

**Q: Do you offer cashless treatment?**
A: Yes! We have tie-ups with Star Health, ICICI Lombard, Bajaj Allianz, and 10+ other providers.

**Q: How do I get my lab results?**
A: Lab results are available within 24-48 hours. You can collect them from reception or receive via WhatsApp.

**Q: Do you offer teleconsultation?**
A: Yes! Video consultation available for ₹800. Call to book.

**Q: What should I bring to my appointment?**
A: Photo ID (Aadhaar/PAN), insurance card (if applicable), list of medications.
