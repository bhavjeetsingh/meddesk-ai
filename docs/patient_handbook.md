## Appointment Policy

### Scheduling
- Appointments can be scheduled online, by phone, or WhatsApp
- We recommend scheduling routine visits 2-3 days in advance
- Same-day appointments may be available for urgent needs

- **24-hour notice** is required for all cancellations
