## Payment Options

### Accepted Payment Methods
- **Cash**
- **UPI:** Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM
- **Credit Cards:** Visa, MasterCard, American Express, RuPay
- **Debit Cards:** All banks
- **Net Banking:** All major banks
- **NEFT/RTGS**

### Payment Terms
- Payment is due at **time of service**
- **GST of 18%** applicable on consultation fees
- Insurance cashless available for eligible patients
- Self-pay patients can claim reimbursement

## How to Avail Cashless Treatment

1. **Bring your insurance card** and photo ID (Aadhaar/PAN/Passport)
2. **Visit our insurance desk** at reception
3. **Pre-authorization** will be initiated (if required by insurer)
4. **Treatment proceeds** after approval
5. **Pay only** non-covered items (if any)

### Documents Required for Cashless
- Insurance card
- Photo ID (Aadhaar Card / PAN Card / Passport)
- Referral letter (if required by insurer)
- Previous medical records (if available)

## Tax Benefits

### Section 80D - Health Insurance Premium
- Up to ₹25,000 deduction for self and family
- Up to ₹25,000 additional for parents (₹50,000 if senior citizen)
- Preventive health checkup: ₹5,000 additional

### Medical Reimbursement
- Keep all bills and receipts for employer reimbursement
- GST invoices provided for all services

## Financial Assistance

For patients experiencing financial hardship:
- **EMI options** available for procedures above ₹5,000
- **Government scheme assistance** at reception
- **Payment plans** for major treatments
