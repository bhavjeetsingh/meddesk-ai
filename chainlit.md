# About MedDesk AI

MedDesk AI is your intelligent virtual health assistant.

## Features
- **Book Appointments** — Schedule visits with specialist doctors
- **Symptom Check** — Describe symptoms and get doctor recommendations
- **Doctor Directory** — View specialists, qualifications, and availability
- **Services & Pricing** — Browse clinic services and fees
- **Insurance Info** — Check accepted providers and payment methods
- **Manage Bookings** — Cancel or reschedule existing appointments
